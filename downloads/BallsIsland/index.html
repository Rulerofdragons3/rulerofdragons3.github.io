Welcome to beautiful Balls Island!

Before I start, mild content warning. This game is intended for adults. There's nothing particularly lewd in it, this game is just really vulgar.

After a sudden change in course, your flight to Hawaii becomes a flight to the beautiful Balls island! Despite your sudden change in plans, you decide to make the most of your situation. Summer is the season for love, and everyone on your flight happens to be single!

What will you do with this opportunity? That's up for you to decide!

Talk with odd characters! Make questionable decisions!

Read morally dubious dialogue! Ignore minor mistakes!

Finally! A visual novel with good dialogue options!

It only gets worse from here! Your island romance is yours to choose in this strange visual novel parody! Download the demo today!
